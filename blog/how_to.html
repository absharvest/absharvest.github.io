Journey to Beekeeping

Beekeeping awakens Nepal's wildflower magic - start your rooftop adventure with one balcony hive. Apis cerana bees pollinate 80% of our crops while you nurture pure, medicinal honey. It's sustainable living, biodiversity protection, and family legacy - turning every rooftop into a thriving apiary.

Nepal Beekeeping Guide

Master Apis cerana - Nepal's native bee that fits any balcony or rooftop! From traditional log hives hanging in countryside to modern Langstroth boxes , harvest pure honey from the hive while pollinating rice, apples, and wildflowers. Perfect for beginners - gentle bees need no big land, just morning sun and NRs(15k - 20k) startup. Transform your home into Nepal's next honey success story.

Care Tips

  • Vital Weekly Rounds: Pop open the hive each week for queen sightings, space expansions, and pest patrols—catch issues early to prevent colony collapse.
  • Spring & Autumn Syrup Boost: Drench your bees with sugar syrup during these key transition seasons for explosive population growth and winter prep fuel.
  • Winter Fortress Defense: Wrap hives tight against freezing winds and frost with insulation tricks—your bees will emerge stronger next spring!

Harvest Honey

  1. Smoke bees gently
  2. Remove ripe frames
  3. Crush, strain, bottle

Bee Types Found In Nepal

Bumblebees (Bombus spp.)

Fluffy, hairy, small colonies. Common: B. haemorrhoidalis (orange tail), B. festivus (yellow bands). Buzz pollination experts.

Apis cerana

Native cavity-nesting honeybee. Slim, brown-black stripes. 60-3,500m across hills. Traditional beekeeping favorite.

Apis laboriosa

Himalayan Cliff Bee. World's largest honeybee. 850-3,500m cliffs. Massive combs, seasonal migration.

Apis dorsata

Giant Honeybee. Large yellow-black bees. Terai to 1,200m. Huge open combs ("Khad mauri"). Wild honey source.

Apis florea

Dwarf honeybee. Smallest species. Lowlands to 1,200m. Tiny combs in bushes. Delicate & fast.

Apis mellifera

Introduced European honeybee. Terai commercial beekeeping. Fails in hills/Himalayas.

Metallic Sweat Bees

Shiny blue-green Halictidae. Garden pollinators with iridescent sheen. Solitary, look like blue-banded bees.

Nepal hosts over 300 bee species! Plant tubular flowers (salvia, lantana, sunflowers) to help pollinators.

About Us

Nestled in the heart of nature, our family-owned business traces its roots to a legacy of passion for honey bees, founded and run by my father. Driven by a deep commitment to conserving traditional beekeeping methods, we blend ancient wisdom with modern insights to safeguard these vital pollinators for future generations.

Our story began decades ago when my father, a dedicated beekeeper, started with just a few hives in our backyard. What began as a humble family venture grew into a thriving apiary, where he passed down time-honored techniques like log hive and natural swarm management. Today, we honor his vision by operating as a true family business, with every member contributing to sustainable practices.

Through immersive workshops, lively events, and rich resources, we empower beekeepers everywhere. Preserving Nepali roots like log hives, wall hives while fusing global flair, we cultivate biodiversity, pure nectars, and unbreakable family bonds under father's watchful eye.

We are continuously experimenting to master the art of queen rearing. And we are curious about different global bee farming practices and actively adapt them to our unique Himalayan environment—join our experiments and workshops to explore and innovate together!

Our Products

Raw Honey Varietals

Raw, unfiltered honeys sourced from local apiaries, offering flavors like mustard(Brassica spp.), wildflower, and Chilaune (Schima wallichii) for health benefits such as antioxidants and natural soothing properties.

Comb Honey

Pure comb honey straight from the hive.

Beekeeping Kits

Starter hives, protective gear, and tools for beginners, including Newton frames and smokers to encourage backyard beekeeping.

Queen Bees & Nucleus Hives

Premium queen bees and nucleus hives (nucs) for beekeepers. Healthy, locally-mated queens with worker bees and frames ready for immediate hive expansion or new apiary starts.
